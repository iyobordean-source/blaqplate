01 — About Blaqplate

Food Made
To Be Remembered.

Blaqplate isn't about getting a plate in front of you fast. It's about the pause right after the first bite — when the pepper hits, the jollof smokes just right, and you look up at whoever you're with because you both need to say something about it.

We cook the dishes you grew up on, built with sharper technique and heavier hands on flavour. Every plate is finished to be photographed, shared, and — more than anything — finished to be eaten with your fingers, your friends, and zero shame.
